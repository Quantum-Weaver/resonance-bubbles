# AudHDities Sanctuary — App Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 4, 2026
**Applies to:** Resonance Bubbles, and the Sanctuary apps it stands beside
(Resonance Echoes, Resonance Compass, and future ones).

> This policy covers the AudHDities **apps**, which are local-first and collect nothing. The AudHDities
> **website / marketplace** (audhdities.com) — which has accounts and payments — has its own separate
> policy. If you only use the apps, only this page applies to you.

## Our promise
Your data belongs to you. We are temporary stewards at most — and for most of what these apps do, we
are not even that, because your data never reaches us. These apps are **local-first**: they run on
your device and keep your information on your device.

## What we collect
**We collect nothing.** We operate no accounts, no servers that receive your content, no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking or telemetry, and no third-party SDKs that phone home. We do not know who you
are, we cannot see your entries, your collection, or your activity, and we have nothing to sell because
we hold nothing.

## Where your data lives
Everything you create — journal entries, moods, tags, notes, settings — is stored **only in the app's
local storage on your device**. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or synced to any cloud. If you
uninstall the app, your device removes that data.

**The game's own data lives there too.** Which stars you have collected, how many of each, your daily
boundary and where you set it, and whether sound is on — all of it is kept on your device and nowhere
else. **Nobody sees your boundary but you.** It is not a score, it is not compared to anyone, and it
is not reported anywhere; it exists so the game can honour a limit you set for yourself.

## Network
**Resonance Bubbles makes no network requests at all.** The game is fully offline: every star, every
sound, and every collection is on your device from the moment you install it. There is no server to
reach and nothing that reaches out.

*(Some sibling apps have narrow, user-initiated exceptions — Resonance Compass can look up cover art
and lyrics when you tap to, sending only that one track's search terms. This app has no such feature
and makes no requests of any kind.)*

## Your control
- **Export:** you can export your data at any time, from within the app, as a single JSON file. One
  envelope carries **all of it** — your journal entries, your folksonomy, and your bubble collection
  together — with the counts written on it so you can see at a glance that the file holds what the
  app shows.
- **Delete / purge:** you can erase all app data at any time. When you purge, it is truly gone — the
  purge is real, not a soft-delete. **Purge and export cover exactly the same ground**, so nothing can
  be destroyed that the export was unable to carry. You may export-then-purge in one action, and the
  export is always completed and in your hands before anything is deleted.

## Special care for neurodivergent data
These tools are built for neurodivergent and vulnerable minds, and the data people entrust to a
personal journal can be deeply sensitive. Our protection is architectural: because that data never
leaves your device and we never receive it, there is nothing for us to misuse, leak, or be compelled
to hand over. Sovereignty is the safeguard.

This applies to the game as much as the journal. How long you play, how often, and where you set your
limit are exactly the kind of numbers other products harvest. **We do not collect them, so we cannot
use them against your attention.** There are no streaks, no notifications calling you back, and no
engagement metrics — not as a policy we promise to keep, but because none of that was built.

## Children
These apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone —
including children — because we do not collect data at all.

## Security
Your data's security rests on your device's own protections. Because nothing is transmitted to or
stored by us, there is no server of yours for us to lose.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because our
model is "collect nothing," any change that would begin collecting data would be announced clearly and
in advance.
