# STORY BLOCK — Resonance Bubbles

*Per the [Story Block Standard](https://github.com/Quantum-Weaver/resonance-standards).*

## WHAT

A collecting game built without compulsion — pop the stars, collect their
words. A sovereign desktop and mobile app (SvelteKit over Tauri v2), built on
the Resonance Echoes base, whose journal features it keeps rather than
strips. 123 stars across 13 collections, a player-set daily boundary, and no
streaks, combos, timers, or locked rarities.

## WHY

Collecting games are usually built to keep the player; this one is built to
let them go. The compulsion loops the genre normally relies on — streaks,
combos, timers, locked rarities, the red badge — are absent by design, so the
game gives away the part that was always the good part: a satisfying pop, and
a word worth keeping. It exists to give *the standalone playable* — a thing
that lived only inside a bigger platform — its own address, on its own terms.

## INSPIRATION

The game began inside the AudHDities platform's tiered `athena` app, detiered
on 2026-07-31. It was named a future want on 2026-08-01, KP's words: *"i kind
of want a stand alone playable game version of the bubble game … not right
now, but future"* — opened the same night as the first resident of
`resonance-awen`'s `games/` room. The seven pop sounds are the chimes'
bubble-wrap family, tuned by ear to KP's own specification: *"like bubble
wrap to a neurodivergent."* The game's laws, its anti-compulsion identity,
and the pop specification are KP's, per `HANDS.md`.
