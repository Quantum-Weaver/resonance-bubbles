## WHAT
*(Definition, purpose, function)*

A collecting game built without compulsion — pop the stars, collect their
words. A sovereign desktop and mobile app (SvelteKit over Tauri v2), built on
the Resonance Echoes base, whose journal features it keeps rather than strips.
123 stars across 13 collections, a player-set daily boundary, and no streaks,
combos, timers, or locked rarities. *(README.md §WHAT IT IS, §THE APP)*

## WHY
*(Need, purpose, problem solved)*

Collecting games are usually built to keep the player; this one is built to
let them go. The compulsion loops the genre normally relies on — streaks,
combos, timers, locked rarities, the red badge — are absent by design, so the
game gives away the part that was always the good part: a satisfying pop, and
a word worth keeping. It exists to give *the standalone playable* — a thing
that had, until 2026-08-04, only ever lived inside a bigger platform — its own
address, on its own terms. *(README.md §WHAT IT IS, §THE STORY)*

## REMEMBERINGS
*(Threads from the past this creation echoes)*

Carries the origin game's five rules whole, **as identity, not settings** —
the boundary is the player's, the game stops kindly, every rarity drifts for

rarity so ranking identities never becomes a game mechanic. Echoes' journal
