# the-bubbles

*The standalone playable — pop the stars, collect their words. One
file (`index.html`), open and play: no build, no account, no
network, nothing leaves the page.*

```
start index.html                 # play (any browser, any machine)
python build.py                  # reseal after editing the set, template, or sounds
```

## The laws (the anti-compulsion identity)

Inherited whole from the origin game (AudHDities athena
BubblePopGame, detiered 2026-07-31 at KP's word) and carried as this
game's identity, not its settings:

- **The limit slider is the point, not a feature** — daily cap 500
  points by default, adjustable 50–2000 by the player alone, saved
  in their own browser. *"The boundary is yours."*
- **"Take a breath" after 50 pops** · cooldown after 15 minutes of
  continuous play — the overlay never shames, never counts down,
  and offers "enough for now" as a first-class choice.
- **Every rarity drifts for everyone** — common to mythic, weights
  60/25/10/4/1; rare is rare, never locked.
- **The reward is the words** — popping a star shows its name and
  its sentence. No streaks, no combos, no timers shown, no red
  anywhere. Rewarding, never shocking.
- **Local-first absolutely** — collections, boundary, and sound
  preference live in localStorage only.

## The set — 55 stars, six collections

The 30 seeded stars carried **verbatim** from the AudHDities base
(athena `bubbles`, read 2026-08-01) plus 25 additions written in the
same voice for the standalone (`bubbles-set.json`, each marked
`addition: true`) — including a new collection, **The Sky Wheel**
(the eight spokes of the turning year, kin to the-sky tool). The
additions stand **offered upward to the online set at KP's gate**;
the JSON is the seed-ready form.

## The voice

The chimes' seven-pop bubble-wrap family — KP's spec ("like bubble
wrap to a neurodivergent"), tuned at his ear — embedded whole as
data URIs (copies of the approved WAVs, never references). No two
pops in a row are ever the same. Sound is a toggle; silence plays
just as well.

## The dress

Cosmic's colors, used freely per KP's word: deep space, star dust,
neurospark, quantum purple, hearth gold. Reduced motion respected.
